What to share

Helpful details for a clear quote

You do not need everything at once — but the more you can share, the smoother the conversation.

  • Idea — bouquet, amigurumi, accessory, or something else
  • Colours — soft palette, bold, or match a photo
  • Size — roughly how big, or for whom
  • Quantity — one piece or a small set
  • Occasion — birthday, thank-you, wedding, everyday
  • Deadline — when you need it by, if known
  • Reference — a photo or sketch if you have one
