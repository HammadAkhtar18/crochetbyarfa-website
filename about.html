About crochetbyarfa

Handmade crochet, made with care — soft colours, thoughtful gifts, and custom work welcome.

Made slowly, with care.

crochetbyarfa began with a simple love of yarn and the joy of giving something handmade. Every bouquet, accessory and amigurumi is stitched by hand — slowly, carefully, and with colour choices that feel warm and gift-ready.

The pieces you see on Instagram are made to bring a smile: lasting flower bouquets, soft hair accessories, playful amigurumi, and useful home crochet such as coasters and potholders. Nothing is mass-produced; each order is treated as its own small project.

Whether you are shopping for a birthday, a thank-you, or a keepsake for yourself, you are welcome to ask questions, request colours, and order through Instagram DM.
